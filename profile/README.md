### 🛠️ Services

- **Kubernetes & GitOps platforms.** Every change goes through Git, every pull request gets its own copy of the app, and the pipeline catches mistakes before production does.
- **Django & Python web apps.** Real tests, migrations checked in CI, and privacy and multi-tenancy designed in from the first model.
- **Data pipelines & scraping.** Scrapers that assume the source will change: scheduled jobs, validation, a snapshot of every failure, clean data in Postgres.
- **AI & LLM integration.** Enrichment with a confidence score on every answer, agents that hand a human a pull request to review, and costs you can predict.

### 🚀 Shipped and running

| Project | What it is | |
|---------|------------|---|
| [**WARN Index**](https://warnindex.com) | US layoff notices from 47 states and DC in one searchable place. Scraped daily, enriched by an LLM, mapped and charted. | **48** jurisdictions, daily |
| [**r12d**](https://r12d.ch) | Privacy-first QR stickers, short links, a photo inventory and lost-and-found for households and small businesses. | **0** visitor trackers |
| [**Flock camera map**](https://fluck.r12d.ch) | A map of 336,000 Flock Safety surveillance devices that doesn't track the people looking at it. | **0** third-party requests |
| [**Restroom QR ad network**](https://pisspooridea.lol) | Venues put QR stickers in their restrooms; ad revenue is split across venues by traffic. | **0** raw IPs stored |
| [**K3s homelab platform**](https://raphaelwieland.com/homelab/) | A small production platform in a closet: Flux GitOps, a review app per pull request, managed Postgres, full observability. | **100+** review-app deploys |
| [**raphaelwieland.com**](https://raphaelwieland.com) | One Django app serving two domains, with CI that refuses to ship a broken deploy. | **2** domains, one image |
